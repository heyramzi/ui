@heyramzi/ui docs site at 1280px. Before is main at 7b36a18 (2.20.0), after is branch design/craft-refresh (TSK-1794). Below them, upsys/app at 1280px: before on ui 2.20.0 (upsys HEAD), after with the branch's packages/ui/src swapped in, in a throwaway worktree on the dev database.

Decisions for Ramzi

  1. /docs step tiles and code blocks. They now sit on the grey page instead of white cards, so the steps read as loose text. My pick: put them back on bg-card with --elev-card, since every other surface on the branch is a white card on grey and these are the one exception. Keep or change?
  2. ThreadEventRecord in thread/*. It's a new feature (a record chip inside an activity line) in the same 180-line diff as the thread restyle. My pick: split it into its own commit and release, so the redesign ships as look only and a chip bug can't hold it back. Keep it in this branch or split it out?
  3. The grey page in upsys is mostly the sidebar. upsys's brand sets cards to linen (L 0.985), so its content panel doesn't change and the light-mode shift lands only on the sidebar and on anything that paints bg-background inside a card. Those spots turn into grey wells (13 sites in the table at the bottom). Fix them all to bg-card when upsys takes the release, or keep the ones that read as wells on purpose (the diagram dashboard tiles, the canvas)?
  4. Square swatches turn round. rounded-xs goes from 4px to 6px, so an 8px square legend swatch becomes a circle and a 14px one nearly does (5 sites). Move them to a fixed rounded-[2px] so they stay squares next to round status dots, or accept round swatches?
  5. Keep xs and md as aliases, or remove them? upsys has 273 rounded-md, 20 rounded-xs and 7 --radius-xs / --radius-md reads. As aliases they cost nothing today. Removing them later breaks all 300 at once, so the codemod (rounded-md and rounded-xs to rounded-sm) should ride the upsys bump itself.
